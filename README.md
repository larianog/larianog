## Research

### Video-based heart rate estimation in intensive care
*Undergraduate thesis · USP · 2025–2026*

Studied **remote photoplethysmography (rPPG)**: estimating heart rate from video by detecting subtle changes in skin color.

- Compared seven deep learning models in a single-subject ICU pilot study.
- Evaluated CNN, Transformer, Mamba, and state space model architectures, including EfficientPhys, PhysNet, PhysFormer, MTTS-CAN, RhythmMamba, ME-chunk, and ME-flow.
- Investigated accuracy, inference time, and generalization from pretrained models to an ICU setting.
- Obtained the lowest mean absolute error with **EfficientPhys pretrained on RLAP: 2.74 BPM**.
- Had a paper based on this work accepted at **CBEB 2026**.

This was a limited pilot study, and its results are specific to the evaluated recording.
